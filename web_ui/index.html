How it works

Three simple steps for a clearer screening workflow.

01

Add a retinal image

Upload a fundus image or capture one with an approved retinal camera. A normal webcam photo is not suitable for clinical screening.

02

Check image quality

RetinaCare checks focus, contrast, field of view, and signal before it provides an automated screening estimate.

03

Review and follow up

Listen to or download the summary, then discuss every result with an eye-care professional.

Private local history

Screening history is stored in the local development database, and summaries can be downloaded directly from the browser.

Quality safeguards

Ungradable images are labelled for retake rather than being used to produce a disease-severity result.

When to seek urgent care

Seek immediate eye care for sudden vision loss, a curtain or shadow, new flashes or floaters, or severe eye pain.

About RetinaCare

Built to support screening, not replace clinical care.

RetinaCare is a local development tool for retinal-image quality checks and diabetic-retinopathy screening experiments. It can use a labeled PyTorch checkpoint when one has been trained; otherwise it clearly identifies the interpretable development baseline. A clinician must confirm every result and decide on care.
